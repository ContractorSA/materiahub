Control and transparency.

This Cookie Policy explains which storage technologies Materia Hub uses, the purposes for which they are used and how you can manage your preferences.

01

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They may be used for essential functionality, to store preferences or to collect aggregated statistical information.

Your consent preference may also be stored in your browser's local storage (localStorage), allowing the cookie banner to remember your decision.

02

Cookie categories

  • Essential: required for basic functionality, security and the storage of your consent preferences. They cannot be disabled through the cookie banner.
  • Analytics: used only after consent to measure website traffic and help improve content through Google Analytics 4.

The website does not use personalised advertising or remarketing cookies unless this is expressly stated in a future update of this Policy and the appropriate consent is requested.

03

Google Analytics 4

With your consent, the website activates Google Analytics 4 to collect aggregated statistics about your visit, such as pages viewed, session duration, device type and general geographic area.

Google Analytics is not loaded before you choose to accept analytics cookies. You may withdraw your choice at any time.

04

Cookies that may be used

  • materia_cookie_consent — stores your preference regarding analytics cookies. Storage: localStorage, up to 12 months.
  • _ga — used by Google Analytics to distinguish visitors. Typical duration: up to 2 years.
  • _ga_<MEASUREMENT_ID> — used by Google Analytics 4 to maintain session state. Typical duration: up to 2 years.

Exact cookie names or durations may be changed by the service provider. The information above is updated when the actual technical implementation changes.

05

Managing consent

During your first visit, you can accept or reject analytics cookies. Rejecting analytics cookies does not restrict your access to the website's content.

You may change your preference at any time by selecting the “Cookie settings” link or button available at the bottom of the website.

06

Browser settings

You can also delete or block cookies through your browser settings. Deleting your stored preference may cause the cookie banner to appear again.

07

Third-party services

Analytics cookies are provided by Google as part of Google Analytics 4. Processing is also governed by Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

FormSubmit, Vercel, GitHub and Papaki may process technical data in order to provide their services, but they are not automatically classified as analytics cookies of this website.

08

Changes to this Policy

This Cookie Policy may be updated when the services, cookies or applicable legislation change. The date of the latest update is shown at the top of this page.

09
